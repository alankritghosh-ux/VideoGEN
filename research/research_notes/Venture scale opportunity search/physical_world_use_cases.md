# Physical-world-intelligence use cases (satellite / weather / public records) with identifiable paying buyers — India-first, software-only founder lens

Research date: 2026-10-17 (all URLs accessed 2026-10-17). Evidence classes used inline: **[Verified fact]** = primary document/pricing page/tender read directly; **[Corroborated]** = ≥2 independent secondary sources or reputable press; **[Estimate]** = my arithmetic from verified inputs, or training-knowledge recollection flagged as unverified; **[Hypothesis]** = reasoned inference; **[Unknown]** = could not establish. "Decision informed" = which diligence question the item bears on.

Method note: ~40 web searches + ~35 page/PDF fetches. The per-turn web-search budget was exhausted before every sub-question was closed; items that could not be searched are listed under Gaps rather than guessed. Several Indian government/portal PDFs (IREDA, RECPDCL, CSE, NSIL) were downloaded and text-extracted locally with pdftotext; figures quoted from them are [Verified fact].

Cross-cutting data facts used by several use cases:
- **ISRO/NSIL imagery pricing (Cartosat-3, 0.28 m PAN):** Rs 3,860 per 17×17 km GEO Orthokit scene and Rs 4,810 per ortho-rectified scene for Non-Government Entities; free for Government Entities. 0.45 m pan-sharpened 10×10 km scene: Rs 2,400 (NGE) / Rs 1,400 (GE). Cartosat-2S 0.6 m: Rs 2,060–6,190/scene. RISAT-1/EOS-04 3 m SAR: Rs 4,890/scene (NGE), free for GE. All commercial IRS data ordering moved to NSIL under Indian Space Policy 2023. — [NRSC/NSIL "Commercial Products Ordering at Bhoonidhi thr' NSIL Interface FAQ v1.0" (file dated 17 May 2024), mirrored at ibm.gov.in](https://ibm.gov.in/writereaddata/files/17210395936694fae952c70Commercial_Products_Ordering_Bhoonidhi_Thr_NSIL_Interface_FA.pdf) [Verified fact; text-extracted]. Implied cost: Rs 4,810 ÷ 289 km² ≈ **Rs 17/km² for 0.28 m ortho imagery** [Estimate, my arithmetic]. Decision informed: data-cost floor for a sub-metre Indian product is tiny vs. Maxar/Pléiades tasking; archive-only, tasking cadence not guaranteed.
- **Cloud cover (monsoon) — quantified only globally, not India-specific:** Whitcraft et al. 2015 (RSE 156:438–447) using 10–13 yrs of MODIS: early/mid growing season is "characterized by both frequent and pervasive cloud extent"; in many agricultural areas "less than half of their 8-day composites would be even 70% clear"; SAR recommended as alternative. — [MODIS abstract page](https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/sci_team/pubs/abstract.php?id=20047) [Verified fact]. Zhou et al. 2025 (IEEE GRSL) on HLS v2.0 for 2022: global mean 69 / median 64 cloud-free 30 m observations per year; tropics have the fewest. — [USGS publication page](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/towards-seamless-global-30-meter-terrestrial-monitoring-evaluating-2022-cloud-free) [Verified fact]. TCS Research notes SW monsoon (mid-June to mid-October) degrades optical observations and uses Sentinel-1 SAR to estimate NDVI — [ISPRS Archives XLII-3/W6 2019](https://isprs-archives.copernicus.org/articles/XLII-3-W6/175/2019/isprs-archives-XLII-3-W6-175-2019.pdf) [Verified fact, from search snippet]. A Chennai-basin study found adding Sentinel-1 to Sentinel-2 raised LULC accuracy by 5.68 pp during monsoon — [Middlesex repository](https://repository.mdx.ac.uk/item/898vy) [Corroborated]. **Gap:** I found no published India-specific count of cloud-free Sentinel-2 scenes per month during June–September. Working assumption for product design: optical revisit should be treated as near-zero for ~3–4 months over peninsular/IGP India; any product promising monthly cadence in monsoon must be SAR-first [Hypothesis]. Decision informed: whether a "monthly optical change detection" promise is deliverable in India.

## UC1. Infrastructure construction progress & milestone verification for lenders/investors (LIE substitution/augmentation; NHAI; Gati Shakti)

### Takeaway
Independent progress verification in India is a real, recurring, regulated spend — but the fee pool is small per project (IREDA pays its LIEs Rs 50,000–2,25,000 **per quarter per site visit**, i.e. ~Rs 2–9 lakh/yr per project), and what lenders buy is a signed disbursement certificate and liability, not imagery. The observable signal (earthworks, module tables, tower foundations, asphalt) is reliably detectable at 0.3–3 m; the certifying decision is not. NHAI already mandates monthly drone video to its own Data Lake; Gati Shakti/BISAG-N already embeds satellite imagery. Entry is as a cheap, remote "between-visits" evidence layer sold to LIEs/lenders, not as a replacement LIE.

### Cited Findings
- **Who pays, how much, how often (India RE):** IREDA's 9 Dec 2022 RFP for empanelment of Lender's Independent Engineers sets a lump-sum fee **per quarter, per visit**: Solar ground-mounted Small (≤10 MW) Rs 50,000; Medium (10–50 MW) Rs 75,000; Large (>50 MW) Rs 1,00,000; Wind Small/Medium/Large Rs 1,26,000/1,80,000/2,25,000; Hydro >25 MW Rs 2,00,000; large rooftop >50 MW Rs 1,26,000; Electric mobility Rs 90,000. Fee "paid by IREDA and debited to borrower's account"; travel/lodging borne by borrower; rates fixed for FY2022-23 to 2024-25; LIE must make at least one site visit per quarter; construction-phase scope includes monitoring actual vs schedule, reviewing drawdown requests vs progress, issuing drawdown certificates, verifying completion status of contracts. — [IREDA RFP-Final28072025.pdf (re-issued 28 Jul 2025, original NIT dated 09.12.2022)](https://ireda.in/images/whatsnewpage/RFP-Final28072025.pdf) [Verified fact; text-extracted locally]. Decision informed: size of fee pool per project; cadence (quarterly) of the decision.
- **Implied fee as % of capex [Estimate, my arithmetic]:** a >50 MW solar project at Rs 1,00,000/quarter ≈ Rs 4 lakh/yr; over an 18-month build ≈ Rs 6 lakh plus travel, against capex of roughly Rs 400–500 crore for 100 MW (capex per MW is a training-knowledge estimate, unverified) → **~0.01–0.02% of project cost**. Decision informed: a software product cannot be priced off LIE fee displacement; it must be priced off lender risk/portfolio scale.
- **Other lenders run their own LIE empanelments:** Bank of India application for LIE empanelment FY26-27 (window 25 May–24 Jun 2026) — [BOI PDF](https://www.bankofindia.bank.in/documents/20121/234301/ApplicationFor_BOI_LIE_Empanelment_FY_26-27.pdf/) [Verified fact of existence; fee annexure not readable — 403]; National Housing Bank notice for empanelment of LIEs (Dec 2021) — [NHB PDF](https://www.nhb.org.in//wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Notice-inviting-Applications-for-Empanelment-of-LIEs.pdf) [Verified fact of existence; content 503]. Decision informed: buyer is fragmented across each lender's panel; LIE firms (SgurrEnergy, ITCOT, etc.) are the channel.
- **LIE assignment profile (what gets monitored):** ITCOT's indicative LIE list (dated 31.12.2025) shows assignments for IOB, Yes Bank, Karur Vysya, Indian Bank, SBI, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank across sugar/distillery (Rs 515 cr), STP PPP (Rs 123 cr), IT SEZ (Rs 534 cr), lithium cell plant (Rs 161 cr), medical college (Rs 386 cr), Kochi Metro Phase 1A/1B (Rs 1,066 cr), Swan LNG FSRU port (Rs 4,069 cr), Sterlite optical fibre plant (Rs 1,417 cr); 37 pages of assignments. — [ITCOT LIE assignments PDF](https://itcot.com/webroot/img/5.%20LIE%20Assignments%20%20with%20Project%20Cost-31.12.2025.pdf) [Verified fact; text-extracted]. SgurrEnergy lists LIE work on a 30 MW PTC India solar project (2024) — [SgurrEnergy](https://sgurrenergy.com/projects/ptc-india) [Verified fact]. Decision informed: LIE scope is heavily indoor/industrial (plants, hospitals) where satellites see only the roofline; linear and area infra (metro, ports, solar, sugar) is the EO-amenable subset.
- **Independent Engineer fee on a PPP highway (comparable):** Dhruv Consultancy received an LoA on 11 Jul 2022 for Independent Engineering Services on the Sikar–Bikaner NH-11 DBFOT section (PWD Rajasthan) for **Rs 4.14 crore excl. taxes, 36 months**; project cost not stated. — [MoneyWorks4Me news](https://www.moneyworks4me.com/company/news/index/id/494794) [Verified fact]; original BSE filing [bseindia.com](https://bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/3770dcac-71da-4bec-9e65-38198a507279.pdf) [403, unverified]. Decision informed: highway IE contracts are ~Rs 1.4 cr/yr — an order of magnitude larger than RE LIE fees; the authority (not lender) pays.
- **NHAI drone mandate:** From June/July 2021 NHAI made monthly drone video recording mandatory for all NH projects at development, construction and O&M stages; contractors/concessionaires record (in presence of the Supervision Consultant's Team Leader) and upload comparative current-vs-previous-month videos to NHAI's "Data Lake" portal; Supervision Consultants comment in the monthly digital progress report; NHAI Project Directors do monthly surveys pre-construction and at completion; videos stored permanently as evidence for arbitration. Cost/who pays not stated (implicitly the contractor). — [Indian Infrastructure, 17 Jul 2021](https://indianinfrastructure.com/2021/07/17/nhai-makes-monthly-drone-survey-mandatory-for-all-nh-projects/) [Verified fact]; [Coordinates, Jul 2021](https://mycoordinates.org/national-highways-authority-of-india-makes-drone-survey-mandatory-for-all-national-highways-projects/) [Corroborated]. NHAI describes Data Lake as a cloud-based AI big-data analytics platform that forecasts delays and likely disputes — [Drishti IAS summary, 23 Jun 2021](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/drone-survey-mandatory-for-all-national-highways-projects/print_manually) [Corroborated]. A Garuda Aerospace case study claiming 3,000 km of NH drone coverage for Data Lake is no longer online (page returns "Case Study not found") [Unknown]. Decision informed: NHAI already has monthly ground-truth-grade drone video; satellite adds little for NHAI itself, but Data Lake video is NOT accessible to lenders/investors — the information asymmetry is between authority/contractor and financiers.
- **PM Gati Shakti / BISAG-N:** the NMP platform is built by BISAG-N, has satellite imagery, geo-tagged project tracking with maker-checker status verification; BISAG-N had contributed 1,450+ GIS layers by Mar 2023; BISAG-N and CAG have used satellite data + AI for geospatial audit of public works (Aug 2023). — [IMPRI policy update](https://www.impriindia.com/insights/bhaskaracharya-national-institute-for-space-applications-and-geo-informatics-bisag-n-powering-tech-driven-governance/) [Corroborated, secondary]; [Drishti PDF](https://drishtiias.com/pdf/1729082853.pdf) [Corroborated]. Decision informed: government-side monitoring is being built in-house by BISAG-N; selling EO progress monitoring to central ministries is a low-probability entry.
- **Global comparable — what construction majors want from satellites:** ESA "Space for Construction Monitoring" use-case annex (18 Nov 2024) with Ferrovial: today "construction progress monitoring is carried out via quarterly drone flights and intermittent survey team trips," creating visibility gaps and high cost; target is satellite-based progress monitoring at **daily-to-weekly** cadence across dispersed sites, a typical linear project spanning 30–50 km²; baseline features: vegetation/earthworks/asphalt change, presence of excavators/bulldozers/cranes, vehicle detection, logistics hubs; stretch: earth-moving volumes (3D). ESA offered zero-equity funding up to €500k for feasibility. — [ESA annex PDF](https://business.esa.int/sites/business/files/Annex%20-%20Space%20for%20Construction%20Monitoring%20Use-Cases.pdf) [Verified fact; text-extracted]; [ESA call page](https://business.esa.int/funding/call-for-proposals-non-competitive/space-for-construction-monitoring) [Verified fact]. Decision informed: proves a Tier-1 contractor's willingness to at least co-fund; does not prove recurring SaaS revenue.
- **Indian bank use of satellites in lending is agri, not construction:** ICICI Bank (Aug 2020) uses ISRO/NASA imagery for farm-credit assessment, piloted 2 yrs, extended to 500 villages then targeting 63,000 villages; assessment in days vs industry 15 days. — [Orissa Post](https://www.orissapost.com/first-time-in-india-this-bank-deploys-satellite-data-for-loan-assessment/amp/) [Corroborated]; [Business Standard, 25 Aug 2020](https://www.business-standard.com/article/finance/icici-bank-deploys-satellite-data-for-agricultural-credit-assessment-120082501219_1.html) [403; cited via search snippet]. I found **no** documented case of an Indian bank/NBFC using satellite imagery to monitor construction/project loans [Unknown].

### Inferences
- Observable vs inferred: at 0.3–0.5 m you can OBSERVE cleared area, foundations, module tables, tower stubs, stringing (partially), road formation/asphalt, crane presence, stockyards; you must INFER % physical completion, cost-to-complete, and whether equipment is installed vs merely delivered — and the LIE certifies the latter [Hypothesis].
- The paying decision is quarterly (IREDA) or monthly (NHAI); the costly error is disbursing against false progress or missing a 6–12-month slippage; a Rs 17/km² Cartosat-3 archive scene plus free Sentinel-1/2 makes a "monthly remote progress note" nearly zero-COGS [Estimate].
- Ground-truth verification by the customer is excellent (the LIE visits next quarter; NHAI has drone video) — which both validates the product and caps what it can charge [Hypothesis].
- Startup entry: adjacent layer sold to LIE firms (cheap white-label evidence appendix) or to lenders' credit-monitoring teams for portfolio-wide early-warning across hundreds of sites, priced per site-month; "overlooked customer" = NBFCs/REIT/InvIT investors who do not get Data Lake access [Hypothesis].

### Gaps
- Fee annexures of BOI/NHB/SBI/PFC/REC/NaBFID LIE panels (BOI/NHB PDFs blocked). Project-cost basis for Dhruv's Rs 4.14 cr IE fee.
- No verified NHAI drone-survey tender values or per-km rates (search budget exhausted before this query ran).
- Whether any Indian lender has a satellite construction-monitoring pilot (none found).
- Commercial EO vendors for construction monitoring (SpaceKnow, Blackshark etc.) pricing: SpaceKnow has "$4M over 2 rounds" and lists construction monitoring as a sector — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/spaceknow/financials) [Corroborated]; no pricing found.

## UC2. Solar farm development & operational monitoring (site ID, construction, vegetation, soiling, flooding, thermal, curtailment)

### Takeaway
Drone thermography is an established, recurring, India-native service (SenseHawk scanned Adani's 780 MW Kamuthi twice in 18 months; Indian vendors recommend at least annual and post-monsoon inspections), but nobody publishes Rs/MW prices — it's quote-based; US references are $400–600/hour for thermal flights. Satellites cannot do module-level thermography; they can do site screening, construction progress, vegetation/flood exposure, and (with weather reanalysis) performance anomaly flags. Software-only entry is the analytics layer on others' drone data or the satellite "portfolio exception" layer — both already contested (SenseHawk/Reliance, Raptor Maps, Sitemark).

### Cited Findings
- **Scale and cadence of Indian drone thermography:** SenseHawk performed thermography of 2.5 million modules at Adani Green's 780 MW DC Kamuthi plant (2,500 acres) in under three weeks; it was the second scan with SenseHawk Therm in 18 months; manual inspection "would take several months." — [Solar Builder, 21 Oct 2019](https://solarbuildermag.com/news/sensehawk-says-it-benchmarked-the-condition-of-this-780-mw-solar-site-in-record-time-less-than-three-weeks/) [Verified fact]. Pricing not disclosed. Decision informed: proves a top Indian IPP pays for recurring aerial thermography (~annual).
- **Indian service-provider spec and cadence (no prices):** Elion (pan-India) offers IEC 62446-3 Level-2 drone thermography; durations: ≤1 MW 4–6 h; 1–10 MW 1–2 days; 10–50 MW 2–4 days; 50–100 MW 4–7 days; detects hotspots (10–40 °C above mean), bypass-diode failure (33% module loss), PID (5–30% string loss), open strings (100% string loss), soiling (2–8% per module); recommends **annual minimum, post-monsoon (Sep–Oct), twice-yearly for plants >5 yrs or high-dust**; conditions ≥600 W/m², wind <7 m/s, 10am–3pm. — [Elion service page](https://elion.co.in/solar-panel-drone-thermography-inspection/) [Verified fact; vendor claims]. Decision informed: decision cadence (1–2×/yr) and the loss magnitudes that justify inspection.
- **US price references:** advanced thermal drone inspection "$400 to $600 per hour"; basic $150–300/h; claimed savings vs manual "$1,074 to $1,717 per MW" (unattributed, from a drone firm). — [Averroes blog, 13 Feb 2025](https://averroes.ai/blog/how-much-does-drone-solar-panel-inspection-cost-2026) [Corroborated weakly; secondary, internally inconsistent per-MW table]. Decision informed: order-of-magnitude for willingness to pay per MW per inspection (~$1–2k/MW gross value; India likely far lower) [Estimate].
- **Software pricing is opaque:** Raptor Maps uses custom pricing driven by MW under management, inspections/year, analytics tier, digital twin; revenue is SaaS subscription plus inspection services; "drone-in-a-box" package reported at ~$50,000 hardware + $2,500/yr service (2023, unofficial). — [pv magazine USA, 28 Mar 2023](https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2023/03/28/drone-in-the-box-for-a-thousandths-of-a-cent-per-kilowatt-hour/) [Corroborated]; [Raptor Maps profile](https://www.trendingaitools.com/ai-tools/raptor-maps/) [weak secondary]. Decision informed: MW-based SaaS is the accepted pricing unit globally.
- **Equipment cost floor in India:** radiometric drone thermal payloads Rs 2 lakh to Rs 18+ lakh; FLIR on DJI M300 RTK ~Rs 14–18 lakh. — [Elion blog, 18 Apr 2025](https://elion.co.in/unlocking-the-power-of-drone-thermography-how-to-use-aerial-imaging-for-efficient-inspections/) [Verified fact; vendor]. Decision informed: hardware-light entry is possible via partnering with the many DGCA-licensed operators.
- **LIE cadence for solar (construction):** IREDA LIE quarterly visits, Rs 50k–1 lakh/quarter (see UC1) [Verified fact]. Decision informed: construction-progress monitoring of solar has an existing paying step but a tiny fee.
- **SenseHawk background:** founded 2018, SaaS, dev centres Bangalore & Abu Dhabi; Series A $2M (2018) — [TechCrunch, 26 Sep 2018](https://techcrunch.com/2018/09/26/sensehawk/) [Verified fact]; [Mercom India](https://mercomindia.com/sensehawk-raises-2-million-in-series-a-funding) [Corroborated]. (Reliance acquisition is widely reported but was not fetched in this session — [Estimate, training knowledge].)

### Inferences
- Observable from satellite: site clearance, table/pile grid emergence (0.3–0.5 m clearly; 3 m PlanetScope shows row structure and % area covered; 10 m Sentinel-2 shows only bulk albedo/NDVI change), vegetation regrowth (NDVI), standing water post-monsoon (Sentinel-1 backscatter drop), large fire scars. Not observable: module-level hotspots, diode faults, PID, soiling at module level (Landsat/ECOSTRESS thermal is 70–100 m), inverter/tracker faults [Hypothesis grounded in sensor GSDs].
- Curtailment detection is better done from public grid data (SLDC/NLDC schedules) plus SCADA, not EO [Hypothesis].
- Customer verification against ground truth is easy and immediate (O&M team walks the string) — good for trust, but the IPP's own SCADA already flags string-level losses, so the satellite layer must prove incremental value at portfolio level [Hypothesis].
- Startup entry: (a) analytics on third-party drone data competes with SenseHawk/Raptor; (b) satellite-based "portfolio exception & site-screening" for lenders/IPPs with 50+ sites is the less-contested adjacent layer [Hypothesis].

### Gaps
- No Indian Rs/MW drone thermography price found (vendors quote on request). No SenseHawk/Skylark/ideaForge-services contract values found.
- Solar site-identification market (land aggregators, developers) not researched (budget exhausted).
- Soiling and flood-loss quantification sources for India not found.

## UC3. Transmission corridor and asset exposure (vegetation/ROW encroachment, flood/landslide/fire exposure)

### Takeaway
In the US/EU this is the most proven EO SaaS category (AiDash: 80+ customers, 20% vegetation-budget reduction claims on 50 cm Pléiades + SAR; Overstory: 6 of 10 largest North American utilities; LiveEO: Deutsche Bahn, E.ON). In India the money currently flows to **survey-stage drone LiDAR** (RECPDCL's June 2026 empanelment tender priced in Rs/km) and tree-compensation processes, not to recurring satellite vegetation subscriptions; I found no Indian utility customer of AiDash/Overstory/LiveEO and no CEA mandate for periodic vegetation surveys. Regulatory driver exists only indirectly (CERC availability-linked tariff, ROW norms); the buyer in India is PowerGrid/TBCB developers' O&M, where labour-based patrolling is cheap.

### Cited Findings
- **Indian spend is at the survey stage, priced per km:** RECPDCL tender RECPDCL/TBCB/Survey/2026-27 (issued 19 Jun 2026; bids due 4 Jul 2026) empanels agencies for 2 years for route alignment, **drone LiDAR & photogrammetry survey of transmission lines** for TBCB projects; prices quoted in **Rs per km of line** for plain (<2,000 m), hilly (2,000–3,000 m) and mountainous (Ladakh) terrain, plus Rs per substation-site identification; L1 rate discovered on GeM and fixed for the empanelment; deliverables include PLS-CADD-ready point clouds, orthophotos, DSM/DTM, GIS database; EMD Rs 2.5 lakh; min turnover Rs 50 lakh. — [RECPDCL LiDAR survey RFP 2.0](https://www.recpdcl.in/assets/data/pdf/LiDAR_survey_RFP2.0_23062026.pdf) [Verified fact; text-extracted]. Decision informed: Indian transmission EO money today = pre-construction survey, bought by the bid-process coordinator, by drone, not satellite.
- **US/EU satellite vegetation products and claimed outcomes:** AiDash case with a Fortune-500 Southern-US utility used archive + tasked Airbus **Pléiades 50 cm** multispectral plus SAR; predicts species growth at span level; claims 20% reduction in annual vegetation budget, 15% reliability improvement, >85% accuracy of a 3–5-yr trim plan. — [UP42 case study, 19 Aug 2020](https://up42.com/case-study/utility-vegetation-management-with-aidash) [Verified fact; vendor claims]. AiDash AWS listing: "over 80 customers worldwide" — [AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-rqnwii5llspea) [Verified fact]. AiDash is India-founded (IIT alumni; most employees in India) — [IndiaAI](https://indiaai.gov.in/article/iitians-startup-aidash-combines-satellite-intelligence-with-ai-to-cater-to-industry-needs) [Corroborated]. Pricing: "customized"/contact-sales — [SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/artificial-intelligence/aidash) [weak secondary]. Decision informed: proof of global willingness to pay for satellite vegetation intelligence; no price point.
- **Overstory:** customers quoted include PG&E (pilot), Holy Cross Energy, Powder River EC, Sho-Me Power, Red Eléctrica (Spain, 10,000 miles); logos for AEP, ComEd, PGE, BC Hydro, Hydro One, Eversource; "trusted by 6 of the 10 largest North American utilities", 50+ IOUs/co-ops; cites vegetation rising from 50% to 75% of overhead maintenance budgets (2015–2020); no pricing. — [overstory.com](https://www.overstory.com/) [Verified fact; vendor page].
- **LiveEO:** customers include Deutsche Bahn, E.DIS, Mitnetz Gas, E.ON, Liberty Utilities, Plains Midstream, First Gas; Westerville Electric testimonial: outage rate 18% → 10%; no pricing. — [live-eo.com](https://www.live-eo.com/) [Verified fact; vendor page].
- **US LiDAR comparable:** 780 miles of 69–345 kV lines surveyed by helicopter LiDAR for PLS-CADD, thermal rating and vegetation analysis (2015); value not disclosed — [T&D World, 10 Nov 2015](https://www.tdworld.com/vegetation-management/article/20965867/lidar-services-contracted-for-vegetation-management-project) [Verified fact]. UK Power Networks found LiDAR could cut field vegetation surveys by up to 80% and its tender cut expected survey cost by ~a third — [isurv](https://www.isurv.com/info/390/features/9675/mapping_preventing_damage_to_overhead_power_lines) [Corroborated weakly]. US driver: NERC FAC-003-3 requires annual vegetation inspection (effective Jul 2014) — [powline.com](https://www.powline.com/vegetation.pdf) [Corroborated].
- **Indian regulatory context:** Minimum conductor-to-tree clearances per MoEF guidance / IS 5613 ≈ 4.0 m (132 kV), 5.5 m (400 kV), 9.0 m (765 kV) [Corroborated from OCR'd MoEF doc — figures to be verified] — [CEA-hosted MoEF guidance](https://cea.nic.in/old/reports/others/ps/pce2/cei/moef_transmission_lines.pdf). CEA Technical Standards Regulations 2022, amended by Gazette on **9 Oct 2025** (revised Schedule VII RoW widths); MoEFCC approved revised RoW norms 10 Jun 2026; CEA advisory (Aug 2026) urges HTLS/monopole/CFCC conductors to narrow forest RoW — **no mention of LiDAR, drones, satellite or periodic vegetation survey.** — [SolarQuarter, 6 Aug 2026](https://solarquarter.com/2026/08/06/cea-urges-transmission-utilities-to-adopt-advanced-technologies-to-reduce-forest-land-diversion/) [Verified fact]; [Power Peak Digest](https://powerpeakdigest.com/moefcc-approves-revised-row-norms-transmission-lines/) [Corroborated]. POWERGRID tree-compensation procedure records each tree in the clearance belt (type, girth, height) under the Telegraph Act — [POWERGRID annex](https://apps.powergrid.in/POWERGRID/docs/ENVIRONMENT/IEER%20CHAMPA-%20KURUKSHETRA/ANNEX.-II%20TREE%20COMPESATION.pdf) [Verified fact]. Decision informed: India has no NERC-style annual-inspection mandate; the economic driver must come from availability-linked tariff and trip avoidance.
- **Availability incentive (training knowledge, unverified in this session):** CERC tariff regulations set a normative annual transmission availability (~98.5% for AC systems) with incentives/penalties around it — [Estimate; cercind.gov.in not fetched]. Decision informed: potential INR driver for ISTS licensees (PowerGrid, Adani Energy Solutions, Sterlite/Indigrid) — needs verification.

### Inferences
- Observable: canopy proximity/encroachment at 0.5 m (species/height only inferred; LiDAR gives height directly), new structures in ROW at 0.5–3 m, inundation (Sentinel-1), landslide scars and burn scars (Sentinel-2, 10 m), seasonal vegetation vigour. Not observable from satellite: sag/clearance in metres with legal confidence, individual hazard trees' health (inferred), conductor condition [Hypothesis].
- Customer verification is cheap (line patrollers walk the span) — strong for trust; but Indian patrolling labour is cheap enough that the satellite layer must sell on prioritisation across tens of thousands of km, not on replacing patrols [Hypothesis].
- Startup entry in India: adjacent layer — ROW encroachment/flood/landslide exposure dashboards for TBCB developers and InvITs (Indigrid, PowerGrid InvIT) whose revenue is availability-linked; or supply the analytics to the drone-LiDAR survey agencies RECPDCL is empanelling. Direct competition with AiDash in India is weak-signal: AiDash itself has not announced Indian utility customers [Hypothesis].

### Gaps
- No Indian utility (PowerGrid, state transcos, Adani, Sterlite) satellite vegetation contract found; no PowerGrid LiDAR O&M tender found (searches ran dry at the portal level).
- AiDash/Overstory/LiveEO contract values or per-km prices — none public.
- CERC availability-linked incentive text not fetched; verify in CERC Tariff Regulations 2024.
- Trip-event penalty data (CEA Grid Standards, NLDC trip reports) not researched.

## UC4. Flood, fire, cyclone, heat risk to infrastructure and insurance (parametric; risk scores)

### Takeaway
Parametric insurance in India is real and growing but small and state/NGO-led: Nagaland pays ~Rs 4.5 crore/yr for up to Rs 50 crore cover (SBI General + Munich Re/GIC Re; triggers on IMD gridded rainfall + 34 state AWS, not satellite); IBISA/ICICI Lombard/SEWA heat covers paid ~$5 per woman to ~50,000 women in May 2024; the Centre began talks on a national parametric programme (Reuters, Oct 2025). Triggers are deliberately built on IMD/AWS data, so a startup's EO-derived index is not the trigger of record; the sellable layer is risk scoring/portfolio exposure for lenders and insurers — for which I found no Indian price evidence.

### Cited Findings
- **Nagaland DRTPS:** India's first state parametric disaster cover; insurer SBI General, reinsurers Munich Re and GIC Re; 3-year MoU (from Aug 2024); premium borne by state with InsuResilience Solution Fund; covers flooding, landslides, droughts. — [Artemis, 9 Aug 2024](https://www.artemis.bm/news/nagaland-renews-parametric-insurance-with-sbi-general-munich-re-gic-re-support) [Verified fact]. Mongabay (20 Jun 2025): annual premium **Rs 4.5 crore for coverage up to Rs 50 crore** (wording also mentions Rs 150 crore over three years — ambiguous); ~Rs 1.06 crore paid out so far; trigger data = **IMD gridded rainfall plus the state's 34 all-weather stations**; 2021 Tata AIG/Swiss Re pilot never paid out due to high threshold and inaccurate data; example payout Rs 25,000 to a Kohima resident. — [Mongabay India](https://india.mongabay.com/2025/06/first-payout-under-extreme-weather-insurance-triggers-relief-and-intrigue/) [Verified fact]. Tiered triggers e.g. 1,500 mm aggregate monsoon rainfall → 10% of tehsil sum insured — [NIDM Nagaland deck](https://nidm.gov.in/pdf/acrc26/Parallel%20Session%20VII/1.%20Nagaland.pdf) [search snippet; PDF 503 — Corroborated weakly]. Decision informed: buyer = state SDMA; data of record = IMD; premium scale ~Rs 4–5 cr/yr per state.
- **Heat parametric (IBISA):** product for women workers (Ahmedabad) with ICICI Lombard as risk carrier and VimoSEWA as distributor; 2023 dairy heat-stress product with Godrej Maxximilk; IBISA raised $3M, India presence since 2023. — [ICMIF](https://www.icmif.org/news_story/ibisa-launches-a-new-parametric-heat-index-product-in-india-to-protect-women-workers-from-heat-induced-wage-losses/) [Corroborated; page 403 on fetch, cited from search]; [Startup Luxembourg](https://startupluxembourg.com/news/ibisa-raises-$3-million-to-scale-parametric-insurance-solutions,-strengthening-climate-resilience-ac) [Corroborated]. Reuters via Insurance Journal (6 Oct 2025): ~50,000 self-employed women in Rajasthan/Gujarat/Maharashtra got payouts when temperatures exceeded 40 °C for a week in May 2024 (~$5 each); Nagaland first payout ~$119,000 (May 2025); Kerala milk federation scheme for cattle heat; **Centre in early talks with insurers/NDMA/GIC Re on a nationwide parametric programme**, considering SDRF funds and utility-bill levies for premiums; option of insurer consortium contracting with municipal corporations. — [Insurance Journal, 6 Oct 2025](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2025/10/06/842685.htm) [Verified fact]. Decision informed: pipeline of public buyers; data providers not named.
- **Premium funding mechanism:** The Insurer viewpoint (10 Feb 2025) on using disaster funds to pay premiums — [The Insurer](https://www.theinsurer.com/ti/viewpoint/parametric-insurance-in-india-using-disaster-funds-to-pay-premiums-2025-02-10) [401 paywall; Unknown content].
- **Design caveat:** IMD heatwave alerts are based primarily on absolute temperature while health risk also depends on humidity — [Prayas (Energy Group)](https://energy.prayaspune.org/our-work/article-and-blog/parametric-insurance-for-extreme-heat) [Corroborated]. Decision informed: index design (not EO) is where value sits.
- **HDFC ERGO** appears only as beneficiary of a Manchester research project on reducing basis risk in weather-index crop insurance — [Univ. of Manchester](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/projects/mitigating-basis-risk-in-weather-index-based-crop-insurance-harne/) [Verified fact of existence]. No HDFC ERGO parametric heat product found [Unknown]. Skymet's parametric role: not found (search budget exhausted) [Unknown].

### Inferences
- Observable: flood extent (Sentinel-1, ~10 m, cloud-independent, 6–12-day revisit), burn scars, cyclone damage (post-event optical), surface temperature (Landsat/MODIS, coarse); inferred: depth, asset damage, income loss [Hypothesis].
- Because triggers of record are IMD/AWS, EO-only startups can sell (i) pre-bind exposure scoring of a lender's or insurer's asset book, (ii) post-event claims triage, (iii) basis-risk audits — none of which have Indian price evidence yet [Hypothesis].
- Ground-truth verification: insurers/lenders can check against claims and site visits; for flood extent the match is good; for loss severity it is weak [Hypothesis].
- Entry: adjacent layer to insurers/reinsurers (GIC Re, SBI General, ICICI Lombard) and to DISCOM/transco asset books; the direct trigger market belongs to IMD/Skymet-type weather-data providers [Hypothesis].

### Gaps
- No evidence of Indian insurers/lenders/DISCOMs paying for third-party flood/heat risk scores (searches on Blue Sky Analytics-type vendors did not run).
- Skymet parametric partnerships; HDFC ERGO parametric; Tata AIG current products — not established.
- Nagaland exact sum insured, triggers, and premium (ambiguity between Rs 4.5 cr/yr and Rs 150 cr/3 yrs) — NIDM deck unavailable.

## UC6. Agriculture: PMFBY/YES-TECH, agri-lending (SatSure), sugarcane, commodity intel

### Takeaway
This is the only Indian use case with a **government-mandated, nationally funded, recurring satellite workflow**: YES-TECH is live in ~100 districts across 9 states with a central FIAT corpus of Rs 824.77 crore funding remote-sensing procurement; satellite yield carries a mandated 30% weight (100% in Madhya Pradesh). But implementation is routed through **state science councils/remote-sensing centres (MPCST, HARSAC, APSAC) mentored by NRSC**, not private vendors, and farmer backlash in MP (CSE, May 2026) shows the detectability limits (empty soybean pods, dense sowing). Agri-lending is the strongest private-sector evidence: SatSure (Bengaluru) counts HDFC, ICICI, Kotak as clients and investors, has a CIBIL co-branded product and a 2025 Perfios integration; ICICI itself went from 500 to a 63,000-village satellite credit rollout in 2020. Prices are not public anywhere.

### Cited Findings
- **YES-TECH framework and funding:** YES-TECH blends satellite-derived yield with CCE data into a single yield at Insurance-Unit level; the FIAT tech fund has a corpus of **Rs 824.77 crore** to finance YES-TECH, WINDS and R&D, including "procurement of remote sensing data, installation and maintenance of weather station networks, platform development" (Cabinet, continuation of PMFBY to 2025-26). — [Anantam IAS summary of PIB release](https://anantamias.com/current-affairs/pmfby-continuation-and-modifications-2025/?pdf=1) [Corroborated, secondary of PIB]. Decision informed: the budget line exists; it is spent via states/TIPs.
- **Scale:** "now live in 100 districts across nine States" (Assam, Haryana, Rajasthan, MP, Maharashtra, AP, TN, Karnataka, Odisha); national rollout from Kharif 2023 after two years of testing. — [ICRISAT ISSCA page](https://issca.icrisat.org/scalable-solutions/yield-estimation-using-technology-yes-tech) [Verified fact]. A GoI deck lists 13 states/UTs — [search snippet; Corroborated weakly].
- **Method and weighting:** semi-physical RUE model on SAR + optical 5–30 m imagery, WINDS weather, CCE anchoring; satellite yield has a **mandated 30% weight**. — [Vassar Labs explainer, 2025](https://vassarlabs.com/yes-tech-explained-how-satellites-estimate-crop-yield-for-insurance/) [Verified fact; vendor]. Madhya Pradesh is "the only major agrarian state to allocate **100 per cent weightage** to YES-TECH" — CSE [Verified fact].
- **Implementation structure and problems (CSE, May 2026):** MP rolled out YES-TECH in 2023 (soybean, paddy kharif; wheat rabi); TIP = **MPCST, mentored by NRSC**; two of five manual-prescribed models (AI/ML + semi-physical), model declared in the season's tender notification; MPCST reports reviewed by NRSC and the YES-TECH committee, uploaded to NCIP; **130,989 ground-truth points as of Aug 2025** (41% soybean); insurers in the three study districts: AIC, SBI General, HDFC ERGO; large soybean farmers report sharply lower payouts and demand rollback, doubting satellites can see empty pods/animal damage; insurers say tech is better than manipulable manual CCEs and claims are now timely; insurers unsure satellites capture yield where seed rate is ~2× recommended; PMFBY dashboard shows total claims in the three districts fell 2021–24 vs earlier. — [CSE report page, 18 May 2026](https://www.cseindia.org/implementation-of-yield-estimation-system-based-on-technology-yes-tech-in-madhya-pradesh-13110) and [PDF](https://www.cseindia.org/content/downloadreports/13110) [Verified fact; text-extracted]. Decision informed: detectability ceiling (biomass ≠ yield for pod crops), political risk, and that the "customer" (state) verifies against CCEs every season.
- **State implementers:** Haryana via HARSAC (deck dated Feb 2026) — [HARSAC PDF](https://hsac.org.in/harsac/Upload/GeoSpatial%20Technology%20for%20Crop%20yield%20Estimation%20(YES-TECH)_20260219153429063.pdf) [exists; too large to fetch — Unknown content]; Andhra Pradesh via APSAC docs (Jan 2023) — [APSAC](https://apsac.ap.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Document2.pdf) [exists; Unknown content]. **No private TIP names or tender values found** [Unknown].
- **Agri-lending (SatSure):** HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and Kotak Mahindra Bank are "simultaneously clients and strategic investors" — [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/startups/satsure-the-bengaluru-startup-thats-getting-indias-biggest-banks-to-invest-in-satellites/) [Corroborated weakly; secondary]. CIBIL Credit & Farm Report with TransUnion CIBIL (2022); Rabo Partnerships alliance for cash-flow lending (Aug 2023) — [iGrow News](https://igrownews.com/satsure-and-rabo-partnerships-unite-to-innovate-agri-finance) [Corroborated]; Perfios–SatSure integration of SatSource/SatCollect into lender workflows (Aug 2025) — [IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/perfios-satsure-partner-to-transform-agri-lending/) [Corroborated; page 403 on fetch]. SatSource covering 630,000+ farm plots in 230+ districts — [AIKosh use case](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home/use-cases/details/satsource_farm_level_intelligence_powering_scalable_agricultural_credit_in_india.html) [page did not load; search snippet only]. NITI frontier-tech story: "30-minute farm loans" — [NITI Aayog](https://frontiertech.niti.gov.in/story/30-minute-farm-loans-60000-lending-opportunities-how-ai-is-transforming-agricultural-credit-sat-sure/) [Corroborated]. ICICI 2020 rollout (see UC1) [Corroborated]. Decision informed: banks pay for farm-level satellite verification in KCC/agri lending; price per report unknown; a SatSure blog claims KCC rules made satellite crop monitoring a fundable item within the farmer's loan limit — [SatSure blog](https://www.satsure.co/?p=13589) [vendor; Unknown reliability].
- **Sugarcane:** ISMA + NFCSF jointly ran a pan-India satellite sugarcane acreage survey for 2011-12 via a hired GIS agency using NRSA imagery — [Geospatial World](https://geospatialworld.net/?p=11481) [Corroborated; old]. MRSAC is running an ongoing project deriving cadastral-level cane area and micro-zone yield within Maharashtra mill catchments — [MRSAC project page](https://mrsac.gov.in/MRSAC/flipbook/project/forecasting-sugarcane-production-at-mill-command-area-in-maharashtra-using-satellite-remote-sensing-amp-ground-based-observation) [Verified fact of existence]. IIRS: sugarcane classification accuracy >92% on LISS-IV, >86% Landsat-8, >83% LISS-III; confusions with maize, banana, poplar, wild grass in early stages; mixed cropping — [ISPRS Archives XLII-3/W6 2019](https://isprs-archives.copernicus.org/articles/XLII-3-W6/229/2019/isprs-archives-XLII-3-W6-229-2019.pdf) [Verified fact]. Decision informed: cane-area mapping is technically mature and buyers (ISMA, mills, state cane commissioners) exist, but recent paid private contracts not found.

### Inferences
- Observable: crop presence/type (10 m, multi-date, ~85–92% for cane/rice/wheat), sowing/harvest dates, biomass/NDVI, flood/drought stress (SAR/optical). Inferred: yield (especially for pod/tuber crops), damage inside the canopy, farmer-level identity of plots (needs cadastral linkage) [Hypothesis].
- Ground-truth verification: strong and institutionalised (CCEs, 130k GT points in MP) — which is exactly why discrepancies become political [Hypothesis].
- Startup entry: direct sale to states as TIP is hard (state RS centres + NRSC occupy it; tenders are per season); adjacent layers: (a) QA/dispute analytics for insurers (AIC, SBI General, HDFC ERGO) who separately ground-truth and contest TIP yields; (b) lender-side plot verification where SatSure is the incumbent; (c) sugar-mill cane-supply forecasting sold to mills/ethanol lenders [Hypothesis].

### Gaps
- YES-TECH TIP tender values, per-hectare or per-district prices, and whether private firms (Vassar Labs, Amnex, RMSI, Agrotech Risk, Sat2Farm) hold TIP contracts — not established.
- SatSure per-report pricing and ARR — not public.
- Agri-commodity trading intel buyers in India — not researched.

## UC8. Other: municipal property-tax mapping; illegal-mining surveillance; (briefly) reservoirs/other

### Takeaway
**Property-tax GIS mapping is the clearest Indian paying buyer in the whole set**: Maharashtra sanctioned Rs 260 crore for 367 ULBs (2017) on an MRSAC satellite base; Hubballi-Dharwad is spending ~Rs 23 crore on a 3D GIS survey (2025) with vendors Genesys and RSI Softech, targeting property-tax growth from Rs 140 crore to Rs 300–400 crore; Kolhapur paid Rs 100 per property (Rs 3.72 crore). It is, however, a tender-driven, field-survey-heavy services market (drone/3D/door-to-door) dominated by Genesys/RSI-type firms. Illegal-mining surveillance (MSS) is built in-house by IBM/BISAG-N on Cartosat — a government capability, not a vendor market.

### Cited Findings
- **Maharashtra:** state cabinet sanctioned **Rs 260 crore** for GIS mapping of all C & D municipal corporations, councils and nagar panchayats (17 corporations + 350 councils/NPs = 367 ULBs); satellite base from the Maharashtra Remote Sensing agency; house-to-house survey; aim to roughly double property-tax recovery from ~Rs 2,000 crore. — [GIS Resources, 17 May 2017](https://www.gisresources.com/maharashtra-government-spend-inr-260-crore-gis-mapping-ulbs/) [Verified fact]; [Geospatial World](https://geospatialworld.net/news/indian-state-maharashtra-sanctions-rs-260-crore-gis-mapping) [Corroborated; 403 on fetch]. Decision informed: ≈Rs 70 lakh per ULB average; state-level buyer.
- **Hubballi-Dharwad (HDMC):** Karnataka's first 3D GIS property survey, estimated **~Rs 23 crore**; mapping building height, floor area, land use, boundaries; current property tax Rs 140 crore → expected Rs 400 crore (Feb 2025 article); tenders zone-wise capped at Rs 2 crore per zone, 12 zones awarded to **Genesys International** and **RSI Softech**, scope includes 3D mapping, ground truthing, property-tax software and 3 years of updates; Centre may give Rs 20 crore incentive. — [The Realty Today, Feb 2025](https://therealtytoday.com/news/technology/hdmc-to-launch-karnatakas-first-3d-gis-survey-for-accurate-property-tax-assessment/) [Verified fact]; [Deccan Herald (2 articles)](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Fhdmc-ramps-up-for-gis-survey-3408534), [DH on Rs 20 cr incentive](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Fgis-survey-may-get-rs-20-cr-incentive-from-centre-3606271) [Corroborated; 403 on fetch, from search snippets]. Decision informed: per-city contract ~Rs 20–25 crore; ROI argument is 2–3× tax uplift.
- **Kolhapur:** property assessment project total Rs 3.72 crore; **Rs 100 per property** per tender norms — [Geospatial World](https://geospatialworld.net/news/municipal-body-in-kolhapur-uses-gis-for-property-analyzing) [Corroborated; 403 on fetch; date unknown]. **Chandigarh:** GIS survey allotted for Rs 1 crore — [Tribune archive](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/chandigarh/house-tax-evaders-watch-out-432964) [Corroborated; old]. **Mumbai 2010 cautionary case:** BMC scrapped a mapping tender when L1 bid was Rs 240 crore vs Rs 32 crore estimate — [Coordinates](https://mycoordinates.org/?p=7117) [Corroborated; old].
- **Mining Surveillance System (MSS):** launched 15 Oct 2016 by IBM with BISAG and MeitY under Digital India; mining-lease khasra maps overlaid on **Cartosat and USGS** imagery; automatic triggers for activity within 500 m of lease boundary; triggers vetted at IBM's remote-sensing control centre, sent to district officials for field verification, compliance via mobile app; functional nationwide for major minerals, states advised to use for minor minerals; coal variant with Khanan Prahari app. — [GKToday, 15 Oct 2016](https://www.gktoday.in/union-government-launches-satellite-based-illegal-mining-detection-system/) [Verified fact]; [GIS Resources](https://gisresources.com/government-launches-satellite-based-surveillance-system-monitor-illegal-mining) [Corroborated]; [Lok Sabha reply](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/700038/1/51179.pdf) [exists; not read]. Decision informed: government builds this itself; vendor opportunity limited to state minor-mineral departments (J&K reviewed a "Real time Mining Surveillance app" built by BISAG-N — [Kashmir Reader](https://kashmirreader.com/?p=263506) [Corroborated]).
- **Reservoirs/water:** India-WRIS already publishes reservoir levels (training knowledge; not fetched) [Estimate]. Not pursued.
- Telecom tower siting, EV-charging siting, warehouse site selection, REIT/lender real-estate construction monitoring: **not researched** (search budget exhausted) [Unknown].

### Inferences
- Property tax: observable = building footprints, new floors (stereo/3D or shadow), roof changes at 0.3–0.5 m; inferred = occupancy, use (commercial vs residential), owner. The city verifies with door-to-door surveys — which is why tenders bundle field survey; a software-only change-detection layer ("which of 4 lakh properties changed since last survey?") sold to the incumbent survey firms or to ULBs for the 3-year update phase is the realistic entry, at Rs 17/km² imagery cost [Hypothesis].
- Mining: observable = pit extent growth outside lease (0.5–3 m), haul roads, stockpiles; inferred = tonnage; government already does this; a startup could sell to lessees for compliance self-audit or to lenders of mining companies [Hypothesis].

### Gaps
- Current (2024–26) ULB GIS tender values beyond HDMC; Kolhapur date; per-property rates in other states; whether ULBs buy recurring change-detection after the base survey.
- MSS trigger counts/outcomes and any state-level private vendor contracts.
