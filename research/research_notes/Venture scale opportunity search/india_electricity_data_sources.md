## Q1. Can a startup obtain 12+ months of 15-minute schedule-vs-actual generation for individual solar/wind plants from public sources? Where, format, gaps?

### Takeaway
Yes, for ISTS-connected (inter-state) wind/solar plants in the Southern and Northern regions: the Regional Power Committees publish weekly Deviation Settlement (DSM) accounts with block-wise supporting data files. SRPC's weekly zip (verified, week 28 Sep–4 Oct 2026) contains one CSV per ISTS RE entity (122 files) with 672 rows of 15-minute `available_capacity, sch_total, act_total, dev, dev_perc, rate, ppa_rate` plus charges; NRPC's weekly `Supporting_files.xlsx` has block-wise `Act_Inj_Gen_Stations`, `DC_Stations`, `Solar_availability` sheets and the DSA page lists weeks from 29-03-2021 to 04-01-2026; WRPC states block-wise data accompanies each weekly statement (secondary). Prayas has already built two full financial years (FY24, FY25) of such 15-min data for 24–40 plants per region from "WR and SR RPC" publications, which is the proof-of-feasibility. Gaps: state-embedded (intra-state) plants are not covered; files are revised; weekly lag; portals are flaky; NRPC format is hard to interpret; licence is unstated.

### Inferences
- A 12+ month plant-level backtest set for ISTS wind/solar is buildable today from SRPC and NRPC weekly files (Estimate: SR ~50 entities and NR ~40+ entities visible in the files; WR adds ~50 if the WRPC zips can be scripted). Each week is a separate zip/xlsx with revisions (R1/R2…), so an ingestion pipeline must de-duplicate revisions and tolerate schema drift (SRPC file names carry "dev2022" = 2022 DSM regulation vintage; NRPC has "supporting file" and "supporting file (New Format)").
- The SRPC `available_capacity` column is the generator's declared available capacity (AvC) per block, which is exactly the denominator in CERC DSM 2024 — so the public files allow reconstruction of deviation %, band exposure and penalty under the new X-factor rules per plant (Verified columns; interpretation is Estimate).
- Coverage is ISTS only. State-embedded plants (most of Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Maharashtra wind) appear only in SLDC/QCA statements that are either not published (Karnataka, Gujarat, TN unverified) or JS-gated per QCA (Maharashtra).
- Pavagada/NP Kunta 33 kV line-wise injection files are a rare public window into sub-plant (feeder-level) metering at a solar park, useful for validating satellite-based plant monitoring.

### Gaps
- Not verified: whether SRPC keeps every historical weekly zip online (one week verified; legacy host srpc.kar.nic.in unreachable), WRPC zip URLs (JS site), ERPC files (host unreachable), and the NRPC "New Format" file structure.
- No RPC site states a data licence or terms of use (checked SRPC/NRPC/NERPC HTML; none found).
- Whether `act_total` is SEM-metered energy at the pooling station (likely, given the "meter_data_meterwise" file) rather than SCADA — Hypothesis.

## Q2. National sources: CEA, NPP, India Data Portal mirror, MNRE, data.gov.in/GODL

### Takeaway
CEA/NPP give daily station- and unit-level data for thermal/hydro/nuclear ≥25 MW (XLS + PDF, ~3-day lag), monthly state-wise RE generation back to April 2016 with a plant-wise ISGS/CPSU sheet (Excel for recent months), monthly installed capacity (2017-01 onward continuous), monthly transmission-line completions and monthly market-monitoring PDFs. A third-party CKAN mirror (India Data Portal, ODC-BY) has already flattened the NPP/CEA daily reports into CSVs from 2017 (unit-level generation and outages) and 2019 (daily plant-wise ISGS RE generation), though its refresh appears to have stopped in mid-2025/2026. CEA's own copyright policy requires prior emailed permission to reproduce; data.gov.in (GODL) was unreachable.

### Inferences
- For thermal/hydro/nuclear, unit-level daily actual vs programme and outage status is fully public with 9 years of history (via IDP CSVs or by scraping NPP XLS daily) — enough for a merit-order/availability model. For RE, the only official plant-level series are monthly (CEA Excel/PDF) and daily (IDP's ISGS CSV mirrored from CEA, 2019–2025) — not 15-minute.
- The IDP mirror's refresh gaps (RE CSVs last modified May 2025; generation/outage May 2026) mean a startup should scrape the primary NPP/CEA files itself rather than depend on the mirror.
- CEA's "prior permission by email" clause is the most restrictive among national portals on paper; in practice CEA data is re-published by NITI, Ember and IDP, but a commercial product should request permission in writing (Estimate).

### Gaps
- Earliest date in NPP `/dgrReports` picker; exact columns of the CEA daily RE report; the content of CEA "Website Policy" terms-of-use beyond copyright; data.gov.in catalogue contents (unreachable).

## Q3. Grid-India (NLDC) and the five RLDCs: what they publish (secondary descriptions; all unreachable from sandbox)

### Takeaway
Grid-India publishes daily PSP (state-wise energy met, shortage, hydro/wind/solar MU) and daily VRE reports as PDF+XLS organised by FY/month on report.grid-india.in, plus weekly/monthly reports, DSM rates and pool-account status, quarterly "operational feedback on transmission constraints" and event reports on wind/solar plant tripping. RLDCs expose public WBES schedule reports (SRLDC), monthly "SCADA vs SEM (RE generators)" MIS and real-time state wind/solar plots (WRLDC), SEM data and pool-account letters (NRLDC). None of this was verifiable directly on 2026-10-17; all items below are search-engine snippets of the primary pages.

## Q9. Which datasets carry redistribution restrictions that would block a commercial product?

### Takeaway
Blocking or needs-a-licence: IEX (personal, non-commercial; no download/extract/distribute), CTUIL (no reproduction without permission), CEA (prior permission by email, no modification, attribution), MSLDC (prior permission even to hyperlink). Attribution-only and safe: Ember, GEM (except CC BY-NC TransitionZero rows), WRI GPPD, Climate TRACE, Mendeley, India Data Portal (ODC-BY), GODL-India datasets (commercial use explicitly allowed, attribution with URL/DOI). Share-alike: OSM (ODbL). Silent (no terms found): RPC sites (SRPC/NRPC/NERPC), KPTCL SLDC, NPP (reproduction allowed except third-party material; silent on commercial use), NITI ICED, Grid-India/RLDCs (unverified).

### Cited Findings
- IEX: "personal, non-commercial purpose"; "must not download, copy, extract or distribute the Content"; attribution required. — [IEX terms](https://www.iexindia.com/terms-of-uses) — Verified fact.
- CTUIL: "may not be reproduced partially or fully, without due permission from CTUIL". — [CTUIL copyright](https://ctuil.in/copyright-policy) — Verified fact.
- CEA: "Users must obtain prior permission by sending an email request"; attribution; no modification; not misleading. — [CEA copyright PDF](https://cea.nic.in/api/websitepolicy/pdf/view/en/2) — Verified fact.
- NPP: reproduction allowed except third-party-marked material; hyperlinking free; framing not permitted; silent on commercial use/scraping. — [NPP help_policy](https://npp.gov.in/help_policy) — Verified fact.
- MSLDC: hyperlink requires prior permission; "All Rights Reserved". — [MSLDC policies](https://mahasldc.in/website-policies) — Verified fact.
- GODL-India: commercial and non-commercial use, derivative works, attribution with DOI/URL/URI. — [OSM wiki GODL summary](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Open_Geospatial_Data_from_Government_of_India) — Secondary (primary gazette on data.gov.in unreachable).
- IDP: ODC-BY. — [IDP CKAN](https://ckandev.indiadataportal.com/api/3/action/package_show?id=power) — Verified fact.
- Ember CC BY 4.0; GEM CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-NC for TZ rows; WRI CC BY 4.0; Climate TRACE CC BY 4.0; OSM ODbL. — see Q7 sources — Verified fact.
- SRPC/NRPC/NERPC/KPTCL: no licence or terms text found in page HTML. — [SRPC](https://srpc.gov.in/commercial/); [NRPC](https://www.nrpc.gov.in/comm/dsa.html); [NERPC](https://nerpc.gov.in/); [KPTCL](https://kptclsldc.in/) — Verified (absence).

### Inferences
- A product that re-sells price curves needs an IEX data agreement (or must derive prices from RPC files, which carry only the DSM normal-rate/ACP series, not full IEX volumes). A connectivity product should present derived analytics with CTUIL acknowledged and seek written permission; CEA permission e-mails are a formality worth doing early. RPC settlement files are published under statutory transparency obligations (CERC DSM regulations) with no stated terms, which is the least-restricted route to plant-level ground truth (Estimate).

### Gaps
- Grid-India/RLDC/SLDC (Gujarat, Rajasthan, TN) terms of use; whether RPC files fall under GODL (Unknown); IEX commercial data-licence pricing.
