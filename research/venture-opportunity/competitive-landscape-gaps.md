# Competitive landscape: where the real gaps are (and where they are not)

Companion to `competitive-landscape.csv` (70 rows, evidence from the Phase B research notes only; access date 2026-10-17). Each gap is classified as **unmet need**, **unattractive market**, **inaccessible data**, **difficult deployment** or **customer indifference**.

1. **Unmet need (real, unserved): ISTS connectivity/GNA intelligence.** CTUIL publishes bay-/substation-wise margins, grant lists and cluster declarations only as PDFs while sitting on a ~128 GW application backlog; no Indian product turns this into a geocoded, versioned substation database with time-to-effective estimates, and US analogues (Nira, Pearl Street/Enverus) exist but have no India plans. Risk: CTUIL's own Connectivity Monitoring Portal/NSWS forms are improving. [CERC EM](https://www.cercind.gov.in/2026/draft_reg/EM_GNA-fourth-Amnd.pdf) | [CTUIL](https://www.ctuil.in/) | [Nira](https://www.niraenergy.com/)
2. **Unmet need: C&I open-access settlement after signing.** The only free decision tool (CEEW) is frozen at FY2021-22 tariffs; Open Access Exchange and developers give pre-signing advisory free; nobody found productises monthly bill/SLDC/banking reconciliation. JMK's Rs 2 lakh OA report proves buyers pay for OA charge data. [CEEW method](https://www.ceew.in/cef/shared/CEEW-CEF-Open-access-landed-cost-calculator-methodology.pdf) | [JMK](https://jmkresearch.com/open-access-ci-re-market-in-india/) | [OAX](https://www.openaccessexchange.com/blog/open-access-renewable-energy-india-2026-state-data/)
3. **Unmet need but early: FDRE/BESS dispatch, availability compliance and IEX bidding.** 1.5x tariff shortfall penalties and 95-97% availability LDs exist, RTM is 54.85 BU (+41%), ReNew admits an intraday price-forecasting gap, yet Manikaran/REConnect market only generic hybrid+BESS F&S and no Indian optimiser appears on Tracxn; ~102 GWh was tendered in 2025; operational grid storage reached about 12.6 GWh by October 2026 (about 80% merchant, concentrated in Adani Green and ACME), superseding the earlier 758 MWh end-2025 figure that counted standalone tendered projects only. [ReNew 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001848763/000119312526325073/rnw-20260331.htm) | [Manikaran](https://www.manikarananalytics.in/) | [Fluence Mosaic](https://fluenceenergy.com/mosaic-intelligent-bidding-software/)
4. **Unattractive market: plant-level RE forecasting / QCA services.** Commoditised at an implied Rs 4k-33k/MW/yr; the two incumbents are profitable on scale (Manikaran Rs 35.9 Cr revenue, 22 staff; REConnect Rs 72.1 Cr) and pooling beats model accuracy; the model layer is free (AIFS, WeatherNext, Open Quartz). [REConnect deck](http://www.cercind.gov.in/2019/expert-group/Stakeholders%20Presentation/REConnect.pdf) | [Prayas](https://energy.prayaspune.org/images/pdf/CERC_suo-motu_proposal_on_X_for_RE-DSM_Prayas_comments_17-10-25.pdf) | [ECMWF](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/about/media-centre/news/2025/ecmwfs-ai-forecasts-become-operational)
5. **Unattractive market: horizontal satellite analytics.** Orbital Insight ($130M raised, distressed sale), Descartes (fire sale), Ursa (still small), Kayrros (sold to a research publisher); survivors own sensors and sell ~80-90% to governments (Planet 59% D&I, BlackSky 89% from four customers, ICEYE sovereign constellations). [Poulson](https://jackpoulson.substack.com/p/exclusive-google-backed-intelligence) | [Planet 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1836833/000119312526119957/pl-20260131.htm) | [BlackSky 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1753539/000175353926000032/bksy-20251231.htm)
6. **Unattractive market: Indian EO analytics.** No Indian space-tech startup crossed Rs 100 Cr revenue in FY25; SatSure (the one proven commercial buyer channel, bank agri-credit) earns Rs 31.5 Cr with a Rs 42.6 Cr loss; profitable players (Suhora, Vassar) are government-facing services shops. [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/magazine/why-indias-space-tech-start-ups-are-stuck-in-a-low-revenue-orbit) | [SatSure financials](https://inc42.com/company/satsure/financials/)
7. **Inaccessible data: plant-level ground truth.** No public per-plant 15-min actuals exist in India; Grid-India/RLDC/SLDC portals returned 503; IMD station data is paid and non-redistributable with 6-hourly updates and no radars in RE-rich areas, so a forecasting product cannot be validated without a design partner's SCADA. [IMD procedure](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/shimla/docs/data-procedure.pdf) | [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/india-s-clean-energy-firms-seek-better-weather-data-as-rules-tighten-ce7d51dedc8af327)
8. **Inaccessible data: public records without APIs.** DILRMP parcels (40.6 crore Bhu-Aadhaar IDs), PM Gati Shakti NMP (1,400-1,800 layers, role-gated) and Parivesh KMLs exist but have no bulk/public API; sub-1 m geospatial data must stay on Indian servers. Power-sector records (CERC/CTUIL/SLDC/IEX) are the exception: public, PDF-bound, parseable. [NeGD case study](https://negd.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Ready-to-publish-PM-Gati-Shakti-NMP-Case-Study-Final-Draft-01-10-2025-1.pdf) | [SCC Online on DST 2021](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2021/03/04/india-geospatial-data-guidelines-2021/)
9. **Difficult deployment: government procurement.** YES-TECH TIP empanelment requires Rs 5 Cr/yr turnover, 10 experts and 20 districts; states route work to MPCST/ISRO/BISAG-N; first meeting to revenue takes up to two years and L1 selection excludes startups. [PMFBY EoI](https://pmfby.gov.in/pdf/EoI_for_empanelment_of_WIP_under_YE_TECH_181023.pdf) | [CSE MP report](https://www.cseindia.org/content/downloadreports/13110)
10. **Difficult deployment: QCA position.** A software-only entrant cannot pool without becoming the registered QCA (one per pooling substation in Karnataka; SLDC is the default agency), and every state has a different portal with no API/FTP submission. [KERC 2026 via Power Peak Digest](https://powerpeakdigest.com/karnataka-renewable-dsm-regulations/) | [REConnect deck](http://www.cercind.gov.in/2019/expert-group/Stakeholders%20Presentation/REConnect.pdf)
11. **Customer indifference: Indian corporates and transmission owners.** Pixxel has 'one or two' Indian customers vs 'tens' in the US and buyers want free pilots; zero Indian satellite-vegetation contracts were found despite AiDash/Overstory's US success, because Indian spend goes to Rs/km drone LiDAR surveys and cheap patrols. [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/magazine/why-indias-space-tech-start-ups-are-stuck-in-a-low-revenue-orbit) | [RECPDCL RFP](https://www.recpdcl.in/assets/data/pdf/LiDAR_survey_RFP2.0_23062026.pdf) | [AiDash case](https://up42.com/case-study/utility-vegetation-management-with-aidash)
12. **Customer indifference: solar O&M / APM.** Consolidated by IBM (Prescinto, which burned ~3x revenue before selling), Reliance/Nextracker (SenseHawk) and Raptor Maps; large IPPs (Adani) build forecasting in-house and buy foreign weather data, so Tier-1 IPPs are not customers for a new vendor. [pv magazine on Prescinto](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2024/10/15/ibm-acquires-prescinto-for-renewables-asset-performance-management/) | [AGEL FY26 AR](https://www.adanigreenenergy.com/-/media/project/greenenergy/investor-downloads/annual-reports/fy26.pdf)
13. **Structural warning on exits and pricing.** Every analytics acquisition found (Solcast-DNV, AutoGrid-Schneider-Uplight, RatedPower-Enverus, Kayrros-Energy Aspects, Orbital-Privateer) had undisclosed terms; the one nine-figure data exit (Spire Maritime to Kpler, ~$241M) went to a distribution-owning intelligence firm, which is the realistic acquirer type for F1-style products. [Uplight](https://uplight.com/press/uplight-to-acquire-autogrid/) | [Spire](https://spire.com/press-release/spire-global-completes-sale-of-maritime-business-debt-eliminated/)
14. **Evidence gaps that still need Phase D work.** Willingness-to-pay for F1 rests on proxies (no developer consultant fees found); no vendor scan confirmed whether any Indian SaaS already reconciles OA bills (F2); whether merchant co-optimisation is permitted under current SECI/state BESS contracts (F3) was not confirmed; QCA rate cards, Pixxel/SatSure pricing and Planet/GEE commercial price lists are all unpublished.

---

**Post-red-team note (coordinator, 2026-10-17).** Items 1–3 above were written before the hostile review and are superseded on three points. (1) Item 1: CTUIL already publishes the connectivity queue as a filterable 636-row HTML table with export and an API-backed status page; only the bay/substation margins are PDF-only, and those PDFs are stamped RESTRICTED under a copyright policy that forbids reproduction without permission. The "unmet need" is therefore narrowed to prediction and alerting, gated on written permission. (2) Item 2: at least four incumbents touch open-access settlement for consumers (Tata Power Trading, Customized Energy Solutions, Open Access Exchange, BridgeIT; rows added to the CSV), so the gap is "no independent, outcome-priced OA settlement product", not "unserved". (3) Item 14: the vendor scan for OA bill reconciliation was completed in the red team (see red-team-review.md section 4). See red-team-review.md for the full counter-evidence.
